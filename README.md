# Dashboard for wind park technician

## Background 

Our team collaborated with a company that wanted to replace their existing mobile application used by wind park technicians. The previous application had usability issues and did not fully support the technicians’ daily workflows.

The client gave us a high level goal for the application but intentionally left the UX/UI direction open, which meant our team was responsible for designing the overall user experience and interface from scratch.

---

## Goal

The goal of the project was to design and develop a mobile application that would better support technicians working in wind parks by improving usability, clarity, and workflow efficiency.

--- 

## My role

I worked as both:

- a backend developer
- and a UX/UI contributor within the project team

In addition to backend development, I contributed to the visual direction and user interface design of the application.

During the design phase, I created and presented UI concepts and visual ideas for the app. The client responded positively to my proposed design direction, and the team used it as the foundation for the final application design.

---

## Outcome 

The final application design was based on the UI direction I presented during the project.

The project gave me hands-on experience in:

- collaborative product development
- UX/UI decision-making
- balancing usability, technical constraints, and stakeholder expectations
